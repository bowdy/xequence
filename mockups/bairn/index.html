Questions

Frequently whispered questions.

Is this real? Please tell me this isn't real.

It is not real. Bairn™ is a parody website — a joke about parental burnout, the genuinely real (and genuinely strange) kidnap-&-ransom insurance industry, and the eye-watering real cost of raising children. We do not kidnap children. Nobody should kidnap children. If you're a burnt-out parent, the actual product you're looking for is called "a babysitter," "grandparents," or "asking for help," all of which are legal.

Will the children be safe?

Hypothetically, they'd be safer than at home — our fictional facility has corner guards on every table and zero glitter. Realistically, they're safe because they never left your house. They're behind you right now, asking for a snack.

What if I miss them?

Statistically, this occurs around day 3, typically triggered by seeing a small sock. Our fictional retention team will play you a recording of a 45-minute tantrum about a banana that was "broken." The feeling passes.

Do you take teenagers?

We tried. They negotiated better terms than us, unionised the holding facility, and now run our social media. Surcharge applies.

Can I pay the ransom in exposure?

You can't pay it at all. That's the whole product.
